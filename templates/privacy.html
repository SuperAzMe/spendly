Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register an account, along with the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as your browser type and general usage activity, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expense history, and generating the summaries and category breakdowns shown in the app. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and font services) to operate the Service. These providers process data only as necessary to deliver their service to us and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
